Business Loans

Flexible financing for small businesses, traders, and entrepreneurs in Kasese — with local service and straightforward terms.

Loan Types

We offer several types of business finance to match different needs. Contact us to discuss which option is right for you.

💼

Working Capital Loan

Cover day-to-day business costs, buy stock, or bridge a cash flow gap. Suitable for traders, retailers, and service businesses that need short-term finance.

🏗

Asset Finance

Finance equipment, tools, vehicles, or machinery that your business needs to operate and grow. Repayments are structured around the asset's productive life.

📈

Business Expansion Loan

Invest in growing your business — open a new branch, expand your premises, or take on a larger contract with the finance to match.

⚠️ Important: Interest rates, loan amounts, repayment periods, and eligibility requirements are confirmed at the time of application and depend on your business profile and creditworthiness. How to Apply

Our application process is straightforward — most applicants receive a decision within a few working days.

1

Enquire

Call, email, or visit our office to discuss your financing needs. We will explain your options and what to prepare.

2

Submit Documents

Gather and submit the required documents (see the list below). We will let you know if anything is missing.

3

Assessment

Our team reviews your application, assesses your business, and may visit your premises or ask follow-up questions.

4

Decision & Disbursement

Once approved, you sign the loan agreement and funds are disbursed. We will explain all repayment terms clearly.

What Documents You Need

Preparing the right documents speeds up your application. Contact us if you are unsure about any item on this list.

All Applicants

  • Valid national ID or passport
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Proof of residence (utility bill or letter)
  • Completed loan application form

Business Applicants

  • Business registration certificate
  • Trading licence (current year)
  • Bank statements (last 3–6 months if available)
  • Brief description of your business and how the loan will be used

Security / Collateral

  • Details of collateral offered (land title, vehicle logbook, etc.)
  • Valuation documents where required
  • Guarantor details (name, ID, contact) if applicable

May Also Be Required

  • Details of existing loans with other lenders
  • Financial statements or cash flow projections
  • Contract or purchase order (for asset or expansion loans)

Note: Document requirements may vary depending on the loan type and amount. We will give you a complete, personalised checklist when you enquire. Do not submit original documents without asking us first — certified copies are usually sufficient.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our loan services. If yours is not here, call or visit us.

Who can apply for a business loan?
Any individual or registered business operating in or around Kasese District can apply. You must be able to demonstrate a genuine business activity and a reasonable ability to repay the loan. We assess each application individually — contact us to discuss your specific situation.
How long does the application take?
Most applications are assessed within a few working days once we have all the required documents. More complex applications, or those requiring a site visit, may take slightly longer. We will keep you informed throughout the process.
Do I need collateral?
Collateral requirements depend on the loan size and type. Some smaller loans may be available with a guarantor instead of physical collateral. We will explain the security requirements clearly when you enquire, before you commit to anything.
What are the interest rates?
Interest rates are confirmed at the time of your application and depend on the loan type, amount, and repayment period. We do not publish rates on the website because they can change and we want to give you accurate information. Please call or visit us for current rates, and we will explain clearly whether any rate quoted is monthly or annual.
Are there any fees besides interest?
Any fees — such as processing fees or early repayment charges — will be disclosed to you in writing before you sign any agreement. We do not charge hidden fees. If you have any doubts, ask us to explain every cost before you commit.
What happens if I miss a repayment?
We encourage borrowers who are facing difficulty to contact us immediately rather than missing a payment without communication. We will work with you to find a solution where possible. The consequences of non-payment will be set out clearly in your loan agreement.
Can I repay early?
Early repayment is generally possible. Any conditions or charges for early repayment will be stated in your loan agreement. Ask us about this before you sign if early repayment is something you anticipate.
