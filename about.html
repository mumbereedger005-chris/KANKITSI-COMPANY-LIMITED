About Kankitsi Company Limited

A local business rooted in Kasese, delivering construction, supplies, and business finance to communities across the Rwenzori region.

Our Story

Building Kasese from the ground up

Kankitsi Company Limited was established to serve the construction, supply, and financial needs of businesses and communities in Kasese District and the broader Rwenzori region. Over the years, we have grown from a small local contractor into a multi-service company trusted by government bodies, NGOs, schools, and private clients.

We understand the unique challenges of operating in Kasese — the terrain, the rains, the distances, and the community ties that make local knowledge indispensable. Our team is made up of people from the region who care about the quality of work we leave behind.

Everything we do — whether it's constructing a school, delivering furniture, or helping a trader access working capital — is guided by the same values: honesty, quality, and respect for the people we work with.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To provide reliable construction, procurement, and financial services that help individuals, businesses, and institutions in Kasese and the Rwenzori region build, grow, and prosper.

🔭 Our Vision

To be the most trusted local partner for construction and business services in Western Uganda — known for quality work, honest dealing, and genuine investment in the communities we serve.

⚖️ Our Values

Integrity in every contract. Quality in every project. Accountability to every client. Respect for every community we work in.

📍 Our Service Area

Primarily Kasese District and the Rwenzori region, with projects undertaken across Western Uganda for clients with regional operations. We work from our base in Kasese Town.
